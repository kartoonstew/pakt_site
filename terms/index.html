  4. Workout rulesRecorded Apple Health workouts are evaluated against your pact’s rules. Every required criterion must pass, and at least one optional criterion must pass; if there are no optional criteria, only the required criteria apply. Presets use duration, calories or heart rate. Manually entered workouts are excluded. Missing measurements stay pending when the result cannot be established. A recording does not independently prove that exercise happened.
  5. Weeks and rule changesWeeks run from Monday to Monday in the pact’s timezone. A workout belongs to the week in which it started. Goal, stakes and qualification changes take effect next period. The 48 hours after a week ends allow delayed data for that week, not extra time to exercise.
  6. Review before a chargeA shortfall charge requires your confirmation of the exact period, rules, workout data and proposed amount. Missing data or a dispute holds the whole pact-period for review. A deadline or an unanswered reminder never creates a debt. Payments remain paused until this billing policy is available.
  7. Joining, leaving and endingYour first joining week is a grace period with neither charges nor winnings. Rejoining does not restart grace. Leaving stops future participation but preserves obligations you already accepted for an existing period. Ending a pact stops future periods; existing periods still need resolution.
  8. Pact durationChoose indefinite, 1, 3 or 6 calendar months. Month terms start at the next full local Monday and finish a complete workout week; review the exact dates before creating or requesting to join. There is no automatic renewal. The final week keeps its 48-hour reconciliation window, and ending does not erase captured obligations.
  9. No eligible winnerThe first fully resolved, otherwise chargeable week with no eligible winner is waived once for the pact’s entire lifetime, with no charge or fee. Later confirmed missed stakes actually collected for a week with no eligible winner go to Pakt, without an additional service fee. Coupons and joining grace remain separate; missing data and disputes do not spend this grace. Ordinary weeks with eligible winners keep the member payout rule. A new pact has its own grace.
  10. Pakt ProFree includes one active pakt. Pakt Pro lets you create or join up to three active pakts, including pakts you own. If Pro expires, existing memberships and obligations stay; new admissions use your current limit. Pro is an Apple auto-renewable subscription. The price and billing period are shown before purchase in Apple’s confirmation. You can manage or cancel renewal through Apple and restore purchases in Pakt. Subscription fees are separate from pakt stakes; a Pro purchase does not activate stake payments.
  11. Monthly passOne monthly pass covers all your active, eligible pact periods when applied. Apply it before a covered period ends. A covered week has no charge and no winnings, even if you meet the workout goal. Joining grace also excludes winnings. A pact joined later is not covered retroactively.
  12. Payouts and separate pactsEligible members who meet the goal without using a pass share the stakes actually collected for that pact-period, after the $1 service fee per charge. Each pact’s rules, stakes and payout pool remain separate. A displayed estimate is not a guarantee of a charge, payout or bank arrival date.
  13. Corrections and supportReport missing, incorrect or deleted workout records for review. Evidence received after a period is finalized is handled as an adjustment; it does not silently rewrite that period or replay a payment. Charges are subject to correction and refund review. Contact support with a payment or workout dispute.
