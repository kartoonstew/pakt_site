Pakt · privacy policy

PRIVACY

Pakt is operated by Swoll Bros LLC. Pakt uses account, workout, group, device and payment information to provide workout accountability and related app services. This policy explains information handled by Pakt and its service providers, including information collected only when you use a particular feature or a provider needs additional verification.

Account, groups and sharing

Pakt uses Sign in with Apple to create and access your account. The information Apple provides can include your name and email address, including an Apple relay address. Pakt uses your account identifier, profile, display name, timezone, group memberships, Pakt titles and rules, and invite/request relationships to run the service.

Other members of your Pakt can see your display name, workout progress and the workout details the app makes available to the group. A signed-in person with a valid invite code can preview the Pakt title and commitment rules before requesting admission. That preview does not include member identities or workout records. Admission requires the owner's approval.

Health and fitness

With your permission, Pakt reads recorded workouts and heart-rate information from Apple Health. It uses workout type, start and end times, duration, heart-rate summaries, calories and qualification evidence to evaluate progress against each Pakt's rules. Pakt uploads workout summaries and related evidence to its service so that it can synchronize progress and review a period. Explicitly manual workouts do not count. Missing measurements or synchronization problems can leave a result pending. A recorded workout or passing rule calculation does not independently prove that exercise happened.

Pakt does not write workouts to Apple Health and does not use your Health information for advertising. You can skip Health access during setup and review or change access in Apple Health. Changing access does not by itself remove information already synchronized to Pakt or delete source records in Apple Health.

Adult paid participation

Real-money participation is limited to adults 18 or older. Pakt records your explicit confirmation of this requirement, the applicable policy version and confirmation time, linked to your account. This feature is a self-declaration. It does not collect your birth date, an identity document or biometric information to verify your age. Stripe may separately request identity information for payment or payout services as described below.

Apple subscriptions

Pakt Pro is a separate Apple auto-renewable subscription. To verify access, restore purchases and process subscription changes, Pakt receives and stores account-linked product and transaction identifiers, an app-account token and subscription status information, including expiry and relevant renewal, refund or revocation changes. Apple processes the subscription purchase. Pakt does not receive your full payment-card information from Apple. Manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account settings; use Restore purchases in Pakt to reconcile access.

Payments, payouts and Stripe verification

Pakt uses Stripe to provide card setup, stake payments and payout-account onboarding. Pakt sends Stripe account identifiers and relevant account contact information, such as email, and uses Stripe customer, payment-method and connected-account references and status information. Pakt maintains financial records needed to explain commitments, charges, distributions, holds, corrections, refunds and recovery. Pakt does not store your full card number in its app database.

Card information is entered through Stripe's payment component. Billing information can include country and postal code. Stripe-hosted payout onboarding can collect contact and address information, payout-account details and information about an individual or business and its representatives. Depending on the account and verification requirements, Stripe may request a date of birth, tax or government identifier, identity or address documents, and photos or selfies. Automated identity verification may use biometric comparison of a selfie and an identity-document photo. These are conditional provider verification processes, not a statement that every Pakt member must submit every item or that Pakt receives biometric templates.

Stripe presents its own notices and any applicable verification choices. Information is used to provide payment/payout services, verify identity, meet provider requirements and prevent fraud. Pakt's integration primarily stores provider references, verification/capability statuses and related financial records. Do not send card numbers, identity documents or selfies to Pakt support. See Stripe's privacy information at https://stripe.com/privacy for its handling of information supplied to Stripe.

Notifications, identifiers and diagnostics

If you enable notifications, Pakt uses an account-linked device notification identifier to send alerts through Apple's notification service. Pakt keeps notification and activity records, including payment/payout event and delivery status information, so it can display app activity and troubleshoot delivery.

Pakt and its hosting providers process account/request identifiers, operational error information and security diagnostics to authenticate requests, prevent abuse and operate the service. Hosted logs may include request/IP and device or app context. Local interface settings are also kept on your device. Pakt's integrated Stripe payment components collect device and interaction information for fraud prevention, app functionality and Stripe's component analytics. This does not mean Pakt uses workout information for analytics or advertising.

Support

If you choose to contact support, Pakt receives the content you send and your contact details. The app's support email includes basic app/build, iOS and environment information by default, not an automatic attachment of your Health or payment records. Support email is handled through the support mailbox provider. Please describe the issue without sending passwords, full card details or unnecessary Health or identity records.

Service providers

Pakt uses Apple for sign-in, Health access, notifications and subscription services; Supabase for hosted accounts, groups, workout summaries and app services; and Stripe for payment, payout and related verification services. Information is shared with providers as needed for the relevant service. Pakt's App Store privacy details describe collected categories, purposes and account linkage, including conditional provider collection.

Deletion, retention and choices

To request account deletion, sign in and open Settings → Delete Account. Unresolved payment or payout activity may need reconciliation before completion. Deletion ends Pakt membership and removes many account, workout, notification and device records. Financial, review/audit and certain group or invite records may remain where needed to reconcile obligations and corrections, with account references removed or detached as provided by the service. Deleting your Pakt account does not delete source workouts from Apple Health or automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

You can manage Health and notification permissions, leave a Pakt, manage Apple subscription renewal and request account deletion. Providers may retain their own records under their policies. This page does not promise a fixed retention period for every category or immediate erasure of every financial record. For questions about particular records, retention or this policy, email paktfitnessapp@gmail.com.
